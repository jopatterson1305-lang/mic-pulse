# MIC Premium Web Design Skill

Build MIC Pulse as a premium East African business, technology, finance and opportunity intelligence platform.

## Direction
Bloomberg editorial discipline + Stripe/Linear product polish + modern African fintech. Deep navy/near-black, electric blue, strong editorial typography, generous whitespace.

Avoid generic AI SaaS aesthetics, purple gradients, excessive glassmorphism, random 3D, stock-looking illustrations and gratuitous animation.

## Motion
Use motion intentionally: scroll reveals, typography transitions, SVG drawing, chart animation, subtle parallax, card interactions and smooth navigation. Prefer GSAP/ScrollTrigger and Lenis. Use React Three Fiber only for high-value moments. Respect reduced motion and performance budgets.

## Architecture
Navigation → Hero → MIC Intelligence → Business → Market Pulse → Technology & AI → Startups → Opportunities → Founders → MIC for Founders → Newsletter → Footer.

## Hero concept
THE INTELLIGENCE BEHIND EAST AFRICA'S NEXT GENERATION.
Supporting idea: business, technology, finance and opportunity intelligence for people building East Africa's future.

## Product areas
Business: company breakdowns, ownership, founders and industries.
Finance: markets, fintech, investment and economic indicators.
Technology: AI, startups and digital economy.
Opportunities: scholarships, funding, grants, events, jobs and competitions.
Founders: editorial profiles, stories and company metrics.
Founder services: VALIDATE → GROW → CONNECT → RAISE.

## Responsive and quality rules
Mobile must be deliberately designed, not merely shrunk. No horizontal overflow. Use semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, accessible contrast and reduced-motion support. Optimize images and lazy-load heavy 3D.
